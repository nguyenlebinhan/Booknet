ABOUT BOOKNET

A shared shelf.
A world of possibilities.

A book opens. A world stays with you.

booknet brings readers, stories and ideas together. We are a community library in Hà Nội: a familiar place to discover a new voice, borrow your next book and make time for reading.

From a first chapter to a lifelong reading habit, our purpose is simple: make good books easier to find, share and enjoy.
