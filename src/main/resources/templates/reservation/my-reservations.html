RESERVATION & PICKUP POLICY

Held for 2 days (48 hours)

Members may hold up to 5 active reservations at a time. Once a copy returns, your reservation switches to Ready for pickup and holds that physical copy for 2 days. If uncollected by the deadline, the hold expires automatically and the copy passes to the next member in queue.

At the Circulation Desk: 1. Present your reservation code & library card. 2. Librarian verifies eligibility and scans your held copy. 3. Enjoy a standard 14-day borrowing period.
