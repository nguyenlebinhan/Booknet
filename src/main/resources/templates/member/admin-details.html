Security & account controls

Sign-in & recovery

Send a secure password-reset link to the account email. Passwords are never visible to administrators.

Session sign-out

End existing sessions after confirmation.

Lock account access

Locking prevents sign-in and ends active sessions. It does not cancel loans, delete history or remove unpaid fines.

Unlock account access

Only email-verified accounts can be unlocked.

Resend invitation

For pending accounts only. A new invitation invalidates the old link after the resend cooldown.
