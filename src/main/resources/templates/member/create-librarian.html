Create user account

Add a member to booknet and prepare a secure invitation to activate their account.

Account identity

Use the person’s name and an email address they can access.

Email must be unique; duplicate checks run when you submit. Only add a phone number when needed for library services.

Role & membership

The assigned role is permanent after account creation.

Choose the access this person needs for their work or membership.

Review required for elevated access

Librarian and Administrator grant staff privileges. Role assignment requires an administrator and is logged.

Creating an account does not issue a membership card. Issue a card separately after the required membership checks.

Access & activation

Send secure invitation email

The user sets their own password using a unique activation link.

The activation link expires after 24 hours. A new invitation can be requested if the link expires.

Account status after creationInvited · Awaiting activationThe invitation link is generated and emailed after account creation.
Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.

Password security: administrators never enter, view or email a plaintext password.

What happens next

  1. Create the account and generate a unique invitation link.
  2. Send the invitation to the provided email address.
  3. The user chooses a password and activates their account.

Before you create

Confirm the name, email address and role. Resolve any duplicate email before creating the account.
