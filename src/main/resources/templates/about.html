OUR STORY & MISSION

Where books bring like-minded readers together

At booknet, we believe that turning a page is more than taking in information. It is the start of a conversation with yourself and a journey towards new perspectives.

Our mission is to make reading a welcoming part of modern city life. booknet brings together the calm of a traditional library and the convenience of digital library services. Whether you are researching, looking for a weekend novel or joining a book discussion, there is a corner here for you.

"A book opens. A world stays with you. A reading space for everyone."

— The booknet library promise

Selected with care

Our librarians carefully review every book on the shelf, choosing thoughtful writing and ideas that stay with readers.

An open and inclusive space

We welcome readers of every age, from students and working adults to older readers, in a friendly and inclusive space.

A connected reading experience

An online catalog, barcode borrowing and returns, QR fine payments and AI-assisted recommendations help readers find their next book.
