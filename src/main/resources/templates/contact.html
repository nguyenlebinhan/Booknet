FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A little help, right here.

A few quick answers before you get in touch

How do I become a booknet member?

Create a free account through Member registration. After registration, your digital card is issued so you can borrow at the library or browse online.

How long can I borrow a book, and can I renew it?

The standard loan period is 14 days per book. If no other reader has reserved it, you can renew for another 14 days through your online account.

How are overdue fines calculated?

Overdue fines are VND 2,000 per book per day. You can pay through VietQR in your account or at the library service desk.

Does the library accept book donations?

booknet welcomes books shared by the community. Our librarians review and catalog donated books in good condition so that other readers can enjoy them.
